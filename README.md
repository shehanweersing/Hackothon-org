## 📌 Project Overview

Surplus LK is a full-stack surplus food marketplace connecting small bakeries, eateries, and home kitchens in Sri Lanka with local consumers to sell end-of-day surplus food at high discounts (40%–70% off) instead of throwing it away.

The platform provides a simple way for food providers to list their unsold food while allowing consumers to discover affordable food available near them.

Surplus LK aims to reduce food waste while creating economic and social value for both food providers and consumers.

---

## 🇱🇰 The Sri Lankan Context

In major Sri Lankan urban centers such as Colombo, Kandy, and Galle, freshly prepared food including artisan bread, pastries, lunch packets, and other meals may remain unsold at the end of the day.

Instead of allowing this food to become waste, Surplus LK provides a digital platform where providers can offer their remaining food at discounted prices before it expires.

### Surplus LK bridges this gap:

- 🏪 **Food Providers** — Recover part of their food preparation costs and clear end-of-day stock.
- 🛒 **Consumers** — Discover quality meals and bakery products at significant discounts.
- 🌱 **Environment** — Help divert edible food from waste streams and reduce unnecessary food disposal.

---

# 🎯 Main Objective

The main objective of Surplus LK is to create a digital platform that connects food providers with consumers so that surplus food can be sold at discounted prices before it becomes waste.

The system focuses on:

- Reducing food waste.
- Providing affordable food to consumers.
- Helping food providers recover costs from unsold food.
- Making surplus food easier to discover.
- Providing expiry and availability monitoring.
- Supporting reservations and pickup confirmation.
- Providing administrative analytics to measure the platform's impact.
- Using AI-assisted functionality to recommend suitable discounted prices.

# 👥 Target Users

## 🏪 Food Providers

Examples include:

- Bakeries
- Restaurants
- Cafes
- Small eateries
- Home kitchens
- Food sellers

Providers can create listings containing information such as:

- Food name
- Category
- Original price
- Discounted price
- Quantity
- Location
- Pickup time
- Expiry time
- Description

---

## 🛒 Consumers

Consumers can:

- Browse available surplus food.
- Search for food items.
- Filter listings.
- Sort listings according to discount or remaining time.
- View food and provider information.
- Reserve food for pickup.
- Confirm pickup.

---

## 👨‍💼 Administrators

Administrators can monitor:

- Available listings.
- Reservations.
- Claimed food.
- Expired food.
- Food saved.
- Estimated money saved.
- Category-level statistics.
- Overall platform activity.

# 🔄 How Surplus LK Works

The overall process is:

```text
Food Provider
      ↓
Create Food Listing
      ↓
Set Discount & Pickup Details
      ↓
Consumer Discovers Food
      ↓
Search / Filter / Sort
      ↓
Reserve Food
      ↓
Pickup
      ↓
Confirm Pickup
      ↓
Food Saved Instead of Wasted
